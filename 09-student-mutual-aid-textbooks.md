# Seattle International Student Mutual-Aid and Textbook Exchange System

## 1. ER Entity List

| Entity | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User | A Chinese international student who seeks or provides assistance. | UW freshman Lily Zhou or second-year graduate student Leo Wang. |
| Textbook Category | An academic classification dictionary for used-textbook search. | Computer science, electrical engineering, business, humanities. |
| Used Textbook | A reusable professional textbook listed by a student. | *Introduction to Algorithms*, third edition, for $45. |
| Purchase Request | A textbook request used to match used books. | A request for UW ECE 201 books under $150. |
| Mutual-Aid Service | A type of peer-provided daily-life support. | Airport pickup, moving help, course-selection advice. |
| Service Order | A completed mutual-aid service order and status. | Leo provides airport pickup for $25. |
| Campus Pickup Point | A campus location for textbook exchanges. | UW Red Square or the SU Library entrance. |
| Textbook Review | Buyer feedback on textbook condition and content. | “Useful highlighted notes,” rated 4.5. |
| Service Review | A rating and feedback for mutual-aid service. | “Punctual and helpful,” rated five stars. |
| Notification | A message for order confirmations and matches. | “An ECE 201 textbook seller has been matched.” |

## 2. Core Business Scenarios

### International Student Users

- List or request used textbooks, exchange them in person, and review textbooks.
- Request or offer airport pickup, moving, and course-selection assistance.
- View textbook transactions, service orders, and reviews.
- Receive matching notifications and connect with peers.

### Platform Administrators

- Maintain pickup points and review inappropriate textbook and service listings.
- Handle complaints and disputes and review marketplace data.
- Maintain textbook categories and mutual-aid service dictionaries.
